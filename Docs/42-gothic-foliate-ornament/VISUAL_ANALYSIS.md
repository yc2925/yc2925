# Visual analysis — REF-017–REF-020

All four images were opened and inspected for Step 14.0. Historical attributions remain unverified; roles are design guidance only.

## REF-017 — Gothic geometric patterns (A)

**Observed:** Black-and-white plate collage — diaper / diamond foliate grids; pointed windows with sub-arches and foils; crocketed pinnacle / gable; nail-head or studded arch fill; quatrefoil mesh; radially symmetrical leaf-scroll medallion.

**Extract for generators:**
- Bilateral and radial symmetry as first-class constraints
- Repeating diaper cells as density / tiling controls
- Crockets as discrete terminal motifs along edges
- Clear hierarchy: host arch / gable → secondary foils → tertiary stipple / mesh

**Do not extract:** Literal leaf color or plant species; treat “foliate” as stylized stone silhouette.

## REF-018 — Biomorphic creature sculpture (B)

**Observed:** Wall-mounted, bone-white / stone-grey assemblage with strict bilateral symmetry. Central spine (tracery → rib cage → antler crown); lateral wing masses of curved branches; cavities and honeycomb lattice; embedded skull-like forms; perforated lace at the base.

**Extract for generators:**
- Dense volumetric branching with cavities (negative space as structure)
- Antler / rib recursion with curling and thickening toward joints
- Layered relief and undercuts
- Grotesque / anatomical variation knobs (creature intensity) without leaving stone materiality

**Do not extract:** Soft tissue, fur, or living plant canopy. Keep output as carved / assembled stone morphology.

## REF-019 — Gothic stone foliate carving (C)

**Observed:** Cathedral interior support — figures at initiation zone; ribs fan upward into perforated cusped tracery (quatrefoil + smaller foils); ribs transform into heavy crocket / leaf curls; deep undercuts; light limestone material reading.

**Extract for generators (primary integration model):**
- Ornament **emerges from** host ribs / edges, not sprayed onto them
- Dual layers: geometric tracery backbone + volumetric crocket foreground
- Load-bearing visual mass despite perforation
- Clear ornament initiation band above figurative / capital zone

**Primary reference for “architectural integration” and stone materiality.**

## REF-020 — Symmetrical grotesque ornament (D)

**Observed:** Three high-contrast symmetrical panels — elongated medallion with oculus void; interlaced X with recursive scrolls; ribbed spindle with C-scrolls and bridges / struts. Stippled texture; cusped / hooked terminals; circular perforations.

**Extract for generators:**
- Mirrored recursive skeleton graphs
- Scroll / spiral growth rules with cusp terminals
- Struts bridging parallel members (structural lace)
- Density control via recursion depth and void ratio

**Do not extract:** Inkblot noise without hierarchy; panels are grammar boards, not meshes to import.

# Reference gaps to collect

The library is strongest in frontal tracery, construction illustrations and ornamental presentation. The following are missing or only partially covered; empty folders remain available for them.

1. **Verified provenance:** artist/photographer, publication, date, location and reuse terms for every image. Check the printed labels on REF-001 and 012 against primary records.
2. **Pointed-arch fundamentals:** dimensioned two-center constructions and documented alternative arch types, including span/rise and center placement.
3. **Circle and foil constraints:** stepwise tangency diagrams, cusp radius rules and minimum web thickness. REF-006 and 008 help but do not fully specify a generator.
4. **Authenticated Rayonnant examples:** dated/site-identified elevations and measured tracery details. REF-011 is a provisional geometry reference.
5. **Complete rose windows:** front elevation, radial subdivision diagram, dimensions and sections. REF-009 and 015 are useful visually but insufficient for an authenticated reconstruction.
6. **Molding profiles and junctions:** measured cross-sections, oblique/back views, intersection treatment and stone-joint details. REF-008, 010 and 013 provide partial coverage.
7. **Vaults and supports:** dimensioned plan plus section, rib springing and boss details, pier bundles and capitals. REF-004 provides only a perspective view.
8. **Full architectural sections:** wall depth, window reveals, buttress connections and floor/roof relationships. Small profile diagrams are not whole-building sections.
9. **Facades and pinnacles:** whole elevation, modular bay relationships and documented assembly details; REF-007 is a canopy and REF-012 a crossing turret.
10. **Materials and lighting:** close-up stone texture with scale, neutral-light material samples and consistent lighting views. Do not bake photographic shadows into geometry.
11. **Contemporary direction:** credited projects, coherent material palettes and multiple views. Confirm whether the desired result follows the monochrome references, the speculative assemblage, or a controlled combination.

For each new reference record its source, feature, dimensions if known, intended use and unresolved uncertainty. Add one original file per reference ID and link secondary categories through metadata instead of duplicating the image.
